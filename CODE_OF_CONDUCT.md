# Code of Conduct

Participants are expected to be respectful, specific, and constructive. Do not
share patient information, recordings, device identifiers, private research
material, credentials, proprietary SDK content, or unsafe clinical claims in
issues, pull requests, discussions, demonstrations, or patches.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate privacy violations, and claims
that misrepresent research software as a clinical device are not acceptable.
The maintainer may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to
protect contributors, patients, research participants, or the project.

For sensitive concerns, use the private reporting route in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
