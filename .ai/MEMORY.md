# Smart Glasses durable memory

## Approved decisions

- Android is the first controller platform.
- HeyCyan is the first hardware target behind a vendor-neutral capability boundary.
- Physical-media and clinical workflows must fail safely on disconnect, interruption, or capability mismatch.
- Surgeon-facing development begins with observation, documentation, checklist support, and tele-mentoring; navigation and AI guidance are separate higher-risk product lines.
- Surgical sessions require explicit preflight gates and a clear-view/safe-stop path that does not depend on cloud or AI availability.
