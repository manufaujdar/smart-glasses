Research method

What the simulator proves—and what it does not

The console sends allowlisted commands to a deterministic local state machine. It is designed to make command, capability, replay, and safe-state behavior reviewable before physical-device integration.

Current evidence

  • Commands fail closed while disconnected or unsupported.
  • Command IDs provide deterministic replay behavior.
  • Capture modes and declared capabilities are enforced.
  • Synthetic disconnect, battery, and indicator faults can trigger safe-stop behavior in the related runtime tests.

Not demonstrated

  • Bluetooth discovery, pairing, command packets, Wi-Fi transfer, or physical-device compatibility.
  • Camera, microphone, display, battery, thermal, cleaning, or network performance.
  • Clinical benefit, diagnostic accuracy, treatment support, navigation, or regulatory compliance.
  • Security or privacy suitability for patient data.

Next validation boundary

Use an authorized vendor SDK and synthetic scenes to run the device-validation protocol. Freeze hardware, firmware, phone OS, SDK, network, test revision, and expected safe states. Do not introduce patient data until institutional privacy, security, clinical-safety, and regulatory review are complete.
