# Scope and non-goals

## Initial users and setting

The initial research user is a trained surgeon or supervised surgical trainee in
a simulation lab. OR staff, remote mentors, privacy officers, infection-control
staff, biomedical engineering, IT/security and clinical safety owners are required
stakeholders before any hospital pilot.

## Prioritized use cases

1. First-person video for a named, consented remote observer or mentor.
2. Hands-free capture of a staged image or dictated procedure note.
3. Glanceable, locally approved checklist prompts that never replace the team pause.
4. Training-session recording with explicit start, visible state, stop, review and deletion.
5. Equipment/setup documentation using synthetic or non-patient scenes.

## Explicit non-goals for the first product track

- no diagnosis, triage, treatment selection or autonomous recommendation
- no anatomical registration, surgical navigation or instrument trajectory guidance
- no patient recognition, biometric identification or always-on recording
- no autonomous EHR/PACS write and no silent background upload
- no AI-generated overlay in the central visual field
- no replacement or alteration of the locally approved surgical safety checklist
- no real patient data in development, demonstrations or automated tests

Any move into a non-goal creates a new intended-use decision and requires its own
hazard analysis, regulatory assessment, human-factors validation, evidence plan and
release approval.
