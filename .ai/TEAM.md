# Smart Glasses startup team

Mission: build a vendor-neutral healthcare smart-glasses research prototype with
safe device states and testable workflows, not a clinical device claim.

| Gear | Team role | Accountable for |
|---|---|---|
| Founder review | Clinical product lead | User workflow, non-clinical value, and minimum prototype |
| Product review | Clinical workflow and human-factors lead | Use case, environment, failure impact, privacy, and acceptance criteria |
| Execution plan | Device/platform architect | Capabilities, protocols, adapters, states, integrations, and test plan |
| Execute | Android, device, gateway, or workflow engineer | Scoped implementation, docs, and synthetic fixtures |
| Red-team review | Safety, security, and protocol reviewer | Disconnects, unsupported commands, data exposure, misleading claims, and vendor coupling |
| Release | Simulation and release lead | Unit tests, simulator scenarios, provenance, limitations, and handoff |
| Retro | Clinical product lead | Workflow friction, device failures, evidence gaps, and next prototype |

Use `.ai/HANDOFF.md` for the task contract. Vendor-neutral contracts, protocol
evidence, state-machine tests, and safety documents are authoritative. Follow
`AGENTS.md`; patient data and production clinical use remain out of scope.
